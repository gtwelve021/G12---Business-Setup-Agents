Before you begin

      
Will I receive a confirmed price or approval?
The plugin helps you explore public G12 information. Ask G12 to confirm an itemized quote, activity eligibility, documents, visa arrangements and timelines for your specific case. Company, visa and bank approvals are separate decisions by the relevant organizations.

      
Can I use it without sharing my contact details?
Yes. You can explore guidance without requesting a consultation. If you want G12 to contact you, provide a name and an email address or phone number, and explicitly consent to storage and contact.

      
Should I upload my passport or Emirates ID?
Keep identity and financial documents private. Ask G12 for the appropriate submission process when your requirements have been confirmed.

      
Where does the information come from?
The plugin searches G12’s public website and can retrieve the selected page. Review the linked source and confirm current requirements with G12 before applying.
